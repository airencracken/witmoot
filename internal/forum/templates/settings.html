Who is this site for?

What happens to the conversations already here?

Each conversation keeps its audience when you change modes. Owners-only conversations stay with owners. Members-only conversations stay behind sign-in. Replies use the conversation's audience.

Open signup lets anyone become a member. Those new members can read members-only conversations on shared boards. Private boards remain limited to their selected members.

These settings apply to the whole site. Use Manage boards to create boards and set access for individual people.

Private mode hides the whole board from visitors. Personal mode also blocks member access. Existing accounts, invitations, and conversations are kept; unexpired invitations work again when joining is enabled.

Changes take effect for new requests and are kept after a restart.
