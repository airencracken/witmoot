# Witmoot 0.8.0

Owners can now look after the people and conversations on their board.

- Suspend or restore a member from **Manage → Members**. Suspension signs them
  out everywhere and cancels their reset links and unused invitations. Their
  messages and permissions stay. Restoring never brings back old sessions or
  links. Owners cannot be suspended.
- Remove a single message after confirming. Its text and image links are
  erased for good, and a placeholder keeps replies and numbering in place. You
  can also replace a conversation's title. Imvault originals, downloads and
  backups are kept.
- Publish house rules and owner contact details in **Settings**. The footer
  links to **House rules & owners**, readable before sign-in. It lists owner
  usernames, never email addresses.
- See suspensions, restorations and removals in the private owner activity log.
- A compact **Manage** menu holds the owner controls and works with a keyboard
  and without JavaScript.
- Account exports mark removed messages `removed: true`, without their old
  text or images.

## Upgrade

Stop Witmoot and back up the whole data directory before replacing the binary.
The database moves from schema 12 to 13 on startup. Older binaries cannot open
the new schema, so going back needs that backup.

After upgrading, review **Settings → House rules and owners**. Owner usernames
appear on the public page even on Private and Personal boards. Message removal
cannot be undone. Suspended members can still read public pages.
