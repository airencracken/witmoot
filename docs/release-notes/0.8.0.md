# Witmoot 0.8.0

Owners can now look after the people and conversations on their board.

- Suspend or restore a member from **Manage → Members**, with a confirmation.
  Suspension signs them out on every device and revokes reset links and unused
  invitations. Posts and board/group permissions are preserved. Restoring access
  never restores old sessions or links. Owners cannot be suspended.
- Remove individual messages after reviewing and confirming. Their text and
  shared image links are permanently erased; a placeholder keeps replies and
  message numbers in place. The opening message's conversation title can also
  be replaced. Imvault originals, existing downloads, and backups are kept.
- Publish house rules and owner contact details in Settings. The footer links
  to **House rules & owners**, available before sign-in. Owner usernames are
  listed there; private account email addresses are not.
- Review suspensions, restorations, and removals in the private owner activity
  history. Group access reports and local account tools show suspension status.
- A compact **Manage** menu keeps owner controls together and works with the
  keyboard and without JavaScript. The account link stays accessible on phones.
  Secondary buttons use their intended style; repetitive prompts and empty-state
  copy are shorter.
- Removed posts are marked `removed: true` in account exports, without their
  former text or image references. Product documentation now distinguishes
  existing features from planned work.

## Upgrade

Stop Witmoot and back up its entire data directory before replacing the binary.
The database automatically migrates from schema 12 to 13 on startup. Existing
accounts remain active, and existing conversations and permissions are unchanged.
Older binaries cannot open the new schema; reverting requires the pre-upgrade
backup.

Review **Settings → House rules and owners** after upgrading. Owner usernames
appear on the public information page even on Private and Personal boards. Rules
and contact details are optional; enter only details you want to publish.

Suspension controls sign-in and private access. Public pages can still be read
without an account. Message removal is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Validation

Regression coverage includes real HTTP permission/CSRF boundaries, concurrent
sign-in and suspension, stale confirmations, generated action sequences, upgrade
and schema constraints, image erasure, export contracts, and injected failures
at every write. Ten mutation checks verify those regressions catch broken
controls. Browser workflows cover the new controls and existing board/group/image
flows with and without JavaScript; the new pages are checked in both themes at
320, 390, 768, and 1280 pixels.
