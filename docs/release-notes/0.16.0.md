# Witmoot 0.16.0

Members have profile pages, linked from their names in conversations. In Your
account, choose **Edit your profile** to add a name, a short bio and up to five
links. Profiles need sign-in, even on a public board. Your username stays the
same.

Profiles never show email, private settings or message history. Account
exports include your profile; community exports leave out bios. Suspended and
deleted members have no profile page.

Schema 19 adds profile storage. Stop the service and back up the whole data
directory before upgrading.
