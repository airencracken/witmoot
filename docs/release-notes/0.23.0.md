# Witmoot 0.23.0

Account settings now use the same **Times and dates** control as the other
Comfyware apps. Open `/account`, choose an IANA timezone such as
`America/Los_Angeles`, and select **Save timezone**. UTC is the default, and
your choice follows you to every device. With JavaScript, **Use this device's
timezone** fills the field; you still choose whether to save. Everything works
without JavaScript.

Dates handle daylight saving and calendar boundaries. Times on the metrics
page follow your choice; its reporting days stay in UTC. Stored timestamps,
exports and machine-readable times stay in UTC.

Saved timezones carry over. No application migration and no export format
change.
